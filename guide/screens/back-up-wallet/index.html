    Back up a wallet

    A wallet's backup: the plan, made on the backup board, then the checklist that carries it out, and the four sheets the board opens. Faraday backs up a wallet, not a key on its own.

    Opened from: Back up (Continue the backup for one left part way) on a wallet's card and on Backups; Back up wallet at the end of Make a new wallet; Back up this wallet at the end of Restore an existing wallet; Change the plan on the wallet's chart; a wallet in Vault contents. Its title is "Back up New wallet 1", or Back up a wallet with more than one wallet loaded, where the wallet's name is a chip that lists the others to choose from. The way back at the top left is named after the card before the one open, else the screen it was opened from: Wallets, Vault contents, or Make a new wallet, where a chip reads Then: Wallets in the wallet's chip's place.

    A single-key wallet just made, its board the standard way: Place 1 tagged Sees the balance and Can spend alone, its Paper keeping "3dca6805 · Words" and Whole; under it No: a single place, the passphrase line, and at the right Add watch-only software and Customize backup.

    The plan

    The plan is up to three cards: Places, the board; Software, where a place is watch-only software; and The plan, the map with its checks. Closed, Places reads "1 place" or "3 places", and The plan "Any one place lost: Yes".

    "A key made here signs only once this backup is done." stands over the board while a key made on this device waits for this backup.

    Places: the board

    A new plan opens on the board with the standard way placed: each seed's words and the whole wallet description on paper, one place per key. Nothing on it moves until Customize backup.

    ControlWhat it does
    a place's cardits name, "Place 1"; its tags, what someone who finds that place alone gets: Sees the balance, Can spend alone, Can spend alone with the vault's passphrase, Spends with 1 more place, "Spends with 2 more places"; once customized, a press on the name makes it a field, and × removes the place
    Paperwhat the place keeps on paper: a seed's words ("3dca6805 · Words"), its SeedQR, Whole (the printed wallet sheet), "Share 1"
    "Stick 2"a stick at the place, named by the number its pill has at the top of the window (the Faraday stick is Stick 1), with its boxes: a vault ("Vault 1 · new", "Vault 1 · sealed", "Vault 1 · sealed · made in the checklist") and Files · not sealed, the files kept outside a vault
    + stickadds an empty stick to the place
    + placeadds a place
    Watch-only softwarethe software place, while there is one: Scanned in, the description scanned into the software, and a stick of its files
    Add watch-only software, Take off watch-only softwarein the right-hand column, before Customize backup: adds or takes off the software place
    Customize backupunder it: makes the board editable; the list of what the backup can keep takes their place

    A chip on the board is one thing kept at one box:

    PartWhat it does
    the name"3dca6805 · Words"; a seed's chip starts with the fingerprint, a passphrase's with its glyph, a vault's with a closed lock
    the formsWords and SeedQR on a seed's paper copy; Text file and QR picture on a file; a press turns one on or off, and the last one on stays on
    the warning marka check under the board names this chip
    the link marka vault kept on sticks at more than one place
    ×takes the chip off that box
    ⋮its menu

    Drag a chip from the list onto a box, or press it and then press the box; a press anywhere else lets it go. A chip from the list is never used up: placing it places a copy. A drop that lets a place see the balance, or brings it nearer a spend, asks first (Keep it or Take it off).

    The list

    Beside the board once it is customized (the right-hand column at 1280 × 800):

    GroupWhat it holds
    Seedseach seed's chip, with how many places keep it at the row's end ("1 place"); Not here for one not loaded
    a seed's codex32 strings"3dca6805 · codex32 w0za · 2 of 3", a chip per string, for a key made as codex32 shares or loaded from strings
    Passphraseseach seed's passphrase, where it has one
    Wallet descriptionWhole; for a multisig that splits, Add partial descriptions to this backup (Remove partial descriptions once added) and What is a share?; for one that does not, why: "Partial descriptions: only for a sortedmulti or tr(NUMS, sortedmulti_a) wallet"
    Vaultseach vault, × to remove it, and + new vault
    the softwareWatch-only software, where the plan has it

    One place found: can spendYes, No, Only with the vault's passphrase

    A drop never refuses: a plan that keeps something unwise shows a line and a mark on the chip. Only what holds the way on stops Continue on The plan: a seed made here kept nowhere, or a vault that holds nothing.

    After the lock that writes the sticks, a change to files and paper is refused: "Files and paper stay as made after the lock; finish the writes, then Back up again to change them".

    The plan

    The map: a box per place, then each vault, the stick of files, the software and On its own device, each listing what it keeps and its tag (secret, sealed, public), then the three lines.

    "Vault 1 holds nothing", "Seed 3dca6805 is kept nowhere"in the warning tone, in place of Make the checklist

    The checklist

    An item for each thing the plan needs, in the order they are done. An item opens once the one before it is done; until then its line reads "After: Copy seed 3dca6805 by hand". Map, the right-hand column at 1280 × 800, shows each place and what it keeps, a dot each, green once that copy is made. Change the plan under the items goes back to the board.

    ItemClosedIts controls

    "Codex32 strings of seed fab6868a"Checked, Not checkedeach string and its place, Check my copy (codex32 shares)
    "Write the passphrase of seed 3dca6805 on paper"Checked, Not checkedShow the passphrase, Hide the passphrase; Check my copy opens Your copy's passphrase, then Compare

    The seeds as filesNot madea row per seed, For the stick once made; Make it
    The wallet sheetA PDF to printBackup sheet, A PDF to print, Make it

    ControlWhat it does
    Your copy's wordsthe words typed back from the paper, "Word 3 of 12", each shown as dots once typed
    Clearempties what was typed
    the line"Word 4 differs from the seed", in the error tone, or Your copy matches the seed in the ok tone

    "3 scans without a match"after three scans that do not match; Keep the words only moves beside the way on

    Once the copy matches, the item reads Checked and the next item opens.

    Backup done

    Once every item is done: Backup done, the three lines, the wallet's chart, and Open the wallet and Change the plan. In Guided mode, a line on copies made outside the plan, with Extra copies, Learn's page.

    The list as a sheet

    Opened from: Add, which takes the list's place on a narrow column. Its title is Add.

    The list, as above. A press on a chip closes the sheet with the chip chosen; then a press on a box places it. Close closes it.

    Keep it or Take it off

    Opened from: a drop on the board that lets a place see the balance it could not before, or brings it nearer a spend.

    A drop at Place 3 that completes the description there: "Share 1 and seed cf2e083d together make the whole description."

    TitleShown when
    "Place 3 can see the wallet's balance"what was dropped completes the description at that place ("The whole description is kept there.", "Share 1 and Share 2 together make the whole description.", "Share 1 and seed cf2e083d together make the whole description.")
    "Anyone with access to Place 1 can spend"the place keeps as many seeds as a spend needs: "Place 1 keeps 2 of the 2 seeds a spend needs."
    "Anyone with access to Place 1 needs only 1 more place to spend"the place is one place from a spend; "… and the vault's passphrase …" where a vault is counted
    ControlWhat it does
    Take it offundoes the drop
    Keep itkeeps it

    the line"The whole description is still kept at Place 1, so the balance can be seen there. Take it off any place where the balance should stay hidden." where it is; "… at Place 1, Place 2 and Place 3, …" where more places keep it
    Take it off every placetakes the whole description off; shown while a place keeps it

    The menu of a chip

    Opened from: a chip's ⋮: a menu under it at 1280 × 800, a sheet on a narrow window.

    A seed's ⋮ in the list, with Place 1 holding Stick 2 and its paper: Add to… lists "Stick 2 · files", "Stick 2 · Vault 1" and New stick; its paper, which keeps the seed already, is not listed.

    ChipMenu
    in the listAdd to…
    on the boardits name; Move to… and Copy to… where there is a box to go to (a share has no Copy to…), and Take off

    Add to…, Move to… and Copy to… list each box that takes the thing and does not keep it already, under each place's name: Paper, "Stick 2 · files", "Stick 2 · Vault 1", and New stick; under the software, Scanned in and its stick's files. A vault's chip lists the sticks it can go on. At 1280 × 800 a press outside the menu closes it; on a narrow window the sheet has Cancel.

    On a narrow window

    The map is a page of the plan, and One envelope per place lists the places. The board is one column of places, with Add in the list's place.

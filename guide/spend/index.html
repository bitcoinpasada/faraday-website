    Spend

    The guide's transactions spend a coin that does not exist: each pays 0.015 BTC to bc1qcr8te4kr609gcawutmrza0j4xv80jy8z306fyu (BIP 84's first test address) from an input of 0.1 BTC. Faraday reads and signs them; the network would refuse them.

    You end with
    a payment from the 2-of-3 of Make a wallet, written in the watch-only wallet as a PSBT, read and signed on Faraday by two keys, and sent back to the watch-only wallet by QR code or on a stick.
    Before you start
    Make a wallet and Receive.
    You'll need
    • Faraday as section 5 left it: New wallet 1 loaded, with keys 3dca6805 and 5d388376 (Can spend).
    • Sparrow on an online computer, with the wallet loaded (step 5.4), and a camera on the PC that runs Faraday. For the stick, a USB stick other than the Faraday stick.
    Time
    about 20 minutes.

    The page Spend from New wallet 1 opens, one card per step.

    Show the wallet QRthe wallet description, as in step 5.4
    Receive address 0the address Sparrow lists first

    The cards below are the steps to come: Write the payment in Sparrow, Read the transaction, Sign it, Send it and Put everything away; once a PSBT is in, Who signs (Add the seeds that can sign while a key is away) and The other signatures join them. The link at the top left is named after the card before the one open (Load the wallet in Sparrow, once the next card is open): it opens that card again, with what was done there kept. On the first card it goes back to the wallet's card.

    6.3Write the payment, and bring it here

    In Sparrow, on the Send tab: the address to pay, the amount and a fee; then Create Transaction, Finalize Transaction for Signing and Show QR. Bring the PSBT to Faraday one of two ways:

    By QR codeOn a stick
    Press Scan the PSBT and hold Sparrow's animated code up to the camera. The line under the picture counts the parts (UR: 2 of 8 · missing 3, 4, …); the camera closes when every part is in. Go to step 6.4.In Sparrow, Save Transaction to the stick. On a stick is grey until a stick is in (Plug the stick in). Plug the stick in: A stick is attached lists what the lock keeps (Kept), what it wipes (Wiped from memory) and what follows (Then, A fresh start, and the stick visit). Press Lock and use stick; Not now keeps the session.

    Lock and use stick wipes the keys and the wallet. What was not in a vault is listed in red, with · nowhere else: here both keys and the wallet, which then come back only from their backups (section 7). With the wallet and its keys in a vault, Unlock brings them back.

    After the lock the stick visit opens:

    Stick visit: On the stick lists the PSBT (payment.psbt) and the wallet's description (new-wallet-1-descriptor.txt), both ticked; In Faraday lists what waits to be written (in the picture, the files of an earlier spend). Press Import 2 files, pull the stick, and load the wallet and its keys again (section 7, or Unlock for a vault). The card then reads Sign the PSBT from the stick, and a bar above it, payment.psbt in Files · Review:

    Press Sign the PSBT from the stick: the review of step 6.4 opens at once. This picture, step 6.5's right-hand column and the stick pictures of steps 6.6 and 6.7 show the wallet as section 7 restores it, named new-wallet-1-descriptor, with key 3dca6805 alone; its PSBT pays a fee of 1,850 sat. The other pictures of this section show the QR way, with both keys.

    6.4Read the transaction

    The card leads with one line, Read what leaves, what comes back and the fee before signing., and Learn: Verifying, which opens Learn's page on what each row proves.

    RowHereMeaning
    Sends0.01500000 to bc1q cr8t … 6fyuwhat leaves the wallet, and to where
    Change0.08497860 to 1/0, Verifiedwhat comes back: change address 1/0 of this wallet, derived again from its keys
    Fee0.00002140, 2,140 sat · 11.3 sat/vBwhat the miners get
    Spends0.10000000, 1 inputthe coins spent
    Txid1757 6707 … d6ee, Unchanged by signingthe transaction's id, the same in Sparrow
    the chipsNo warnings, Mainnetnothing found to warn of; the network

    Compare the address, the amount and the fee with what was typed into Sparrow. Show the table lists each input and output whole; Hide the table closes it:

    Press Continue. Step 6.8 has what the card shows when something is wrong.

    Verified proves the change goes back to this wallet. Nothing proves the address paid is the one meant: Faraday cannot know it, so compare it with the payee's.

    6.5The keys that sign, and Sign it

    The next card, Who signs, lists the wallet's keys; with a key away it is Add the seeds that can sign:

    Both keys hereOne key here
    3dca6805 and 5d388376 read Signs here, cf2e083d Not here; 2 of 2 signatures here and Can spend.3dca6805 reads Signs here, the others Not here; 1 of 2 signatures here · 1 more needed. Scan a key loads the second key (step 6.6); Collect a signature takes a copy signed elsewhere (Multisig with other people and devices).

    With both keys here, press Continue; with one, step 6.6 first.

    Press Sign with 3dca6805, 5d388376. Signing cannot be taken back: press it only when step 6.4 read right. The next card, The other signatures, reads 2 of 2 and closes by itself.

    6.6The second key

    Both keys sign on this Faraday. With one here, load the second before signing: on Add the seeds that can sign, press Scan a key and hold that key's SeedQR up to the camera, as in Multisig with other people and devices, step 7 (Type the words instead for a key with no SeedQR).

    Scan a keyThe second key in
    Scan a key for new-wallet-1-descriptor lists the keys still away (Key 2 · 5d388376, Key 3 · cf2e083d); Hold the QR in front of the camera.Key 2 added · Can spend, in a bar at the foot of the page; the row of 5d388376 reads Signs here, and the card 2 of 2 signatures here and Can spend.

    Then Continue and Sign with 3dca6805, 5d388376: one press signs with both keys, and The other signatures reads 2 of 2.

    Both wait For the stick as well (Remove takes one off). Under the cards, Nonce check · 2 signatures valid · 2 recomputed here says the two signatures were checked here.

    Signed PSBTFinished transaction
    In Sparrow, on the same transaction: Scan QR, hold it up to the webcam, then Broadcast Transaction. UR and BBQr are the formats; 2/s to 5/s the speed; Small, Medium and Large the size. Done closes it.Text · one code, for a wallet or a block explorer that reads a whole transaction; UR, BBQr and Parts are the formats.

    The finished transaction is safe to hand to anyone: changing where it pays breaks its signatures. Whatever broadcasts it should report the txid Faraday showed (1757 6707 … d6ee); if it reports another, what was sent is not what was signed here.

    On the stick way, the files take the PSBT's name (payment-signed.psbt, payment-final.txn):

    Send itFiles
    Both cards read For the stick; under Nonce check, one row per signature, Valid · nonce recomputed, low R. Decode it, Show the raw hex and Show the finished transaction as QR follow.Files lists them under For the stick, each Ready, with Waiting for a stick. Plug a stick in: the stick visit writes them. In Sparrow, open the signed PSBT from the stick (File → Open Transaction → File…), then Broadcast Transaction.

    Then Put everything away:

    It lists the files waiting for the stick (scanned-1-signed.psbt, scanned-1-final.txn). Lock wipes the session and keeps them; plug a stick in and the stick visit writes them (Write 2 files). Then Power off. Start over begins another spend.

    6.8When the transaction is not right

    The card of step 6.4 says so in its rows and under them, each warning in its own words, in red for danger. A PSBT opened from Files (its Sign) opens Sign a transaction, the same review:

    A fee of 0.012 BTCAnother wallet's PSBT
    fee 1200000 is 80% of the amount sent and fee rate is about 6349 sat/vB, then Hold to pay this fee. This transaction, on the right, sums it up beside the signatures: "Not signed here: see Transaction", its Sign with off until the fee is held.Wallet reads No loaded wallet matches; the change is a second Sends row and, under This transaction, Change reads None, with output 1 claims to be change of a wallet you are using, but the script is not that address and Faraday does not sign this transaction. Make it again in the coordinator without what is marked above. The panel reads "Not signed here: see Transaction", its Sign with off.
    On the cardMeaningDo
    A second Sends row, and "output … claims to be change of a wallet you are using, but the script is not that address"an output claims to be change but does not derive from this wallet's keys; it is shown as a paymentdo not sign: the "change" may pay someone else
    "fee … is …% of the amount sent"the fee is a large share of the amount, or above 0.01 BTC; Review stops at Hold to pay this feedo not sign; write the payment again in Sparrow with a lower fee. Hold only for a child paying for its parent, a large batch or a consolidation
    "fee rate is about … sat/vB"1,000 sat/vB or morethe same
    Wallet with No loaded wallet matches; on its card, "No loaded wallet matches this transaction. Change cannot be verified until its wallet is loaded."the PSBT spends from a wallet not loaded hereload the right wallet, or the right PSBT
    "input … spends from a wallet of several keys that is not registered here; register the wallet to sign", with Register this wallet under itthe wallet this PSBT spends is not registered here, and the PSBT names its cosigners' keyspress Register this wallet: check the shape (2 of 3, P2WSH, sorted), the cosigners and the one marked Yours, then hold Hold to register; the card can then be signed
    the same, with "Load this wallet's descriptor to sign" under itthe wallet is not registered here and the PSBT does not carry its keysload the wallet's descriptor (Files, a code, or Restore an existing wallet)
    Amount unchecked on Spendsan input's amount is only stated, not proved by its previous transactiondo not sign a transaction of several inputs; Faraday refuses one, with no way past
    No key in this session signs this transactionnone of the keys here is one of the transaction'sload the wallet's keys

    Declining costs nothing: press Cancel at the top right, and nothing is signed.
